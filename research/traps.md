### 0.1 Important corrections to the project brief

1. **The reference grammar is NOT Τζάρτζανος** (that is Latin). For Ancient Greek 2026-27 the Ministry lists [PRIMARY]: **Οικονόμου Μ., «Γραμματική της Αρχαίας Ελληνικής»** (ΙΤΥΕ Διόφαντος), **Μουμτζάκης Α. Β., «Συντακτικό της Αρχαίας Ελληνικής»** (Α΄, Β΄, Γ΄ ΓΕΛ), and **Ζαμάρου-Μήτσης-Παπανδρέου «Εγχειρίδιο Γλωσσικής Διδασκαλίας»**. Every Greek rule and label in the app should be checked against these (and the Gymnasium books Αρχαία Α΄, Β΄, Γ΄ Γυμνασίου).
2. **The taught-text curriculum changed**: the 2026-27 book is **«Αρχαία Ελληνικά, Φιλοσοφικός Λόγος» (Κοπιδάκης, Πατρικίου, Λυπουρλής, Μωραΐτου) + «Φάκελος Υλικού»**. All 2022-2026 papers draw from this philosophy-themed set (Aristotle, Plato, Plutarch). There is no Thucydides/Xenophon taught text.
3. **Translation is only 20 of 100 marks and grammar+syntax only 20 of 100.** The exam is dominated by interpretation of the taught text (50 marks), so it is very different from Carpe (where grammar+syntax = 60). See section 1.

## 1. The exam, exactly [PRIMARY: papers 2023-2026; 2022 from key]

All four papers I read have the same skeleton (marks sum to 100; 3 hours; earliest exit 10:00):

| Part | What | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| **Α. Διδαγμένο κείμενο** (12-20 lines of Greek, with a short Modern Greek translation of the unit) | | **50** |
| Α1 | Comprehension, closed-type: true/false (2024: *with citations*; 2026: *no citations*), matching columns (2025), "to which word does this pronoun refer" (2025, 2026), rhetorical-device matching (2023) | 10 |
| Β1 | Interpretive question on the taught text (open answer) | 10 |
| Β2 | **Parallel text** (a Modern Greek passage): find similarities/differences or synthesize, "with references to both texts" | 10 |
| Β3 / Β4 | One is **etymology/vocabulary** (find cognates in the text for Modern Greek words, 2023/2026; fill blanks with cognates, 2024/2025). The other is a **closed question on the introduction** (author's life/work, philosophy): T/F, multiple choice or matching | 10 + 10 |
| **Γ. Αδίδακτο** (Attic prose, 12-20 lines, with a one-sentence context note and 2-4 glossed words) | | **50** |
| Γ1 | **Translate 8-10 lines into Modern Greek** | **20** |
| Γ2 | Comprehension: "Πώς περιγράφονται...", "Με ποια επιχειρήματα...", "Ποιες σκοπιμότητες..." | 10 |
| Γ3 | Grammar: forms (Γ3α + Γ3β, 6+4 marks or similar) | 10 |
| Γ4 | Syntax: Γ4α identify syntactic roles of 6-7 bold words (6-7 marks) + Γ4β a transformation/identification task (3-4 marks) | 10 |

Instructions on every paper [PRIMARY]: write the subject on the cover and the date at the top of the answers; **do not copy the questions**; notes on the paper are not marked; **blue or black pen only**; **"Κάθε απάντηση τεκμηριωμένη είναι αποδεκτή"**; 3 hours.

Changes from 2023 [PRIMARY-ish: alfavita 2023 article]: one of the two 2-mark questions on the taught text was dropped and its marks moved to the translation (translation max went from 8/20 to 10/20, i.e. 20/100 now); the translated passage grew from 5-6 to 8-10 lines.

Third marker: a script is re-marked by a third teacher when the first two markers differ by more than 12 marks (out of 100) [search summary; CNN 2026 describes the process, I did not confirm the number for this subject specifically].

Overall: "demanding with several difficulties". (Poukamisas/CNN the same day said "quite accessible": the groups disagree.)
- **2025 post-exam dispute (esos.gr comments, [WEAK])**: the ΚΕΕ indicative translation was criticized for rendering the participle ποιησάμενος (the sentence "ἐὰν … ἄξω") as hypothetical instead of temporal-prior; commenters (anonymous) argued over whether ἄξω is a future indicative (several) or an aorist subjunctive, and whether ὀψομένη is "the same voice" as ἰδεῖν (active) since ὄψομαι is middle. Lesson [INFERENCE]: **the voice terminology ("στην ίδια φωνή") hides deponent-like futures**; teach futures of ὁράω (ὄψομαι), λαμβάνω (λήψομαι), μανθάνω (μαθήσομαι), etc. as middle and check what the school book accepts.
- **2026** (Poukamisas, Διακρότημα, ΕΕΦ-style commentary by teachers):
  - Taught: "expected unit", "no special difficulty"; for the first time **no textual references were required in Α1** (Δ. Νάτσης); Α1α item 1 was a "trap for hasty students" (the criterion between good and bad πολιτεία is the lawgivers' success at habituating citizens, not "ἕξεις" as such: read, do not associate) [TUTOR commentary by Ζώτου]; Β1 needed the pair γένεσις-φθορά; Β2 the pre-Aristotelian idea that justice is the main "social virtue".

Νάτσης). Lesson [INFERENCE]: the app should accept both readings where the school books allow both and explain which is preferred; students should always write the evidence/reason ("τεκμηριωμένη").

## 5. Marking behaviour that costs marks [TUTOR / PRIMARY instructions]

- "Κάθε απάντηση τεκμηριωμένη είναι αποδεκτή" is on every paper: a documented alternative is accepted. For syntax and interpretive questions, **write the evidence** (the Greek words and the rule name).
- Do not copy the question (it is unmarked time); write the question number only; one pen colour; notes on the paper are not marked [PRIMARY].
- Closed questions 2024: T/F **had to be supported with textual references**; 2026: not required [PRIMARY]. Always check the instruction line (the cheapest marks).
- Open questions are scored by **components** (typical split seen in the paper wording: Β1 2026 = 7 + 3; Α1β 2026 = 4 + 2; Β1 2024 = 2 + 8; Γ4 = 6/7 + 4/3). Answer both parts explicitly; **label the parts** (α, β) in your notebook.
- The tutor keys write form + role + governing word, e.g. «ἀποροῦντα: κατηγορηματική μετοχή, εξαρτώμενη από αισθητικό ρήμα (Ὁρῶ), αναφέρεται στο αντικείμενο «σε»; συντακτικά κατηγορηματικός προσδιορισμός στο «σε»» (2025). Pattern: **term + clause/phrase type + what it depends on**. The school books (Μουμτζάκης) name the term; the app must use those names.
- Marker variability for Ancient Greek: no number found [NOT FOUND]. Third marker at >12 marks of 100 difference.

## 6. Trap catalogue, built from the real papers and keys

### 6.1 Reading the question (cheapest marks)
Wording features seen in the papers:
- **"στην ίδια φωνή / στον ίδιο χρόνο / στο ίδιο γένος / στον άλλο αριθμό"** (2023 ἐπιμεληθῆναι: same tense **and** voice; 2025 ἰδεῖν: future participle same voice → ὀψομένη; 2025 ἄξω: aorist β΄ subjunctive same voice; 2026 φησιν/περιοικοῦσι: same person/number).
- **"στον ίδιο τύπο"** (2024: *perfect in the same form*: κατῆγεν→κατῆχε, **not** κατήγαγε).
- **"το β΄ ενικό της προστακτικής του ίδιου χρόνου"** (φανῆναι: the aorist β΄ passive imperative **φάνηθι**, not a α΄ aorist).
- **"θετικός και συγκριτικός"** (2023 ῥᾷστον asked for two forms).
- **"τον ίδιο τύπο στον συγκριτικό / υπερθετικό βαθμό"** of an adjective (ἰδίαν → comparative of the feminine accusative form).
- **Γ3α 2025: first FIND the third-declension nouns in the quoted passage** (ἰδεῖν is not one, γυναῖκας is): the search is part of the task.
- **Γ4α asks "πλήρως"** (2025: «να χαρακτηρίσετε πλήρως συντακτικά») = role + type + governing word.
- **Closed questions with traps** (2026 Β3: "static state vs activity"; 2025 Β4: "15th letter", "δραματικές εξελίξεις/εκδιώχθηκε"; 2024 Α1: "avoided wars because of Aristotle's advice"): negative or extreme words ("όλοι", "μόνο", "εκτός από") often make the statement false [INFERENCE from the T/F items; verify with more items].

### 6.2 Translation of the unseen (Γ1, 20 marks)
Observed difficulty in real passages (what the tutors/ΕΕΦ flagged):
1. **Indirect speech after an άνω τελεία** (2025): the speech-introducing verb (εἰπεῖν) is far from the contents; find it first.
2. **A conditional not clearly formulated** (2025 «ἐὰν … ἄξω σε»): protasis ἐάν + aorist participle/subjunctive; a participle inside the protasis (ποιησάμενος) may be temporal, modal or hypothetical; check the sense in the context (Vice proposes a way, so "making me your friend" reads best, but tutors disagreed).
3. **Long period with relative clauses and μέν…δέ contrast** (2026 «ὑμᾶς μέν … τοῦτον δέ»): the accusative subjects of the two infinitives (ὑμᾶς … ζημιῶσαι / τοῦτον … ἀπογράψαι) must be paired before translating.
4. **ὥστε opening a sentence** («ὥστε τίς ἂν ἀπετόλμησε …», 2026): here ὥστε is "so, therefore" introducing a main clause with ἄν + aorist indicative (past potential/unreal: "who would have dared"). Compare ὥστε + infinitive in 2025 (consecutive): «ὥστε λευκοτέραν … δοκεῖν φαίνεσθαι». [GENERAL on the meaning; the passages are PRIMARY]
5. **ὡς in at least five roles in the exam passages**: comparative "as" (2026 ὡς οὗτός φησιν), causal/subjective-cause participle (2026 ὡς ἀφανίσαντα, ὡς ἐπεργαζόμενον), predicate "as/being" (2026 ὡς ἐπιμελητὴς), directional "to" before a person (2024 ὡς τὸν Τισσαφέρνην), temporal "when" (2025 ὡς δ' ἐγένοντο πλησιαίτερον), plus indirect statement/final in other passages [GENERAL]. A table of ὡς with an example each is a must for the app.
6. **ἄν**: with a past indicative = unreal past (2026 ἂν ἀπετόλμησε, «Πῶς δ' ἄν … ἐπεχείρησα»); with subjunctive = ἐάν; with optative = potential [GENERAL].
7. **μή vs οὐ**: 2026 «μήθ' ὡς ἐπεργαζόμενον … μήθ' ὡς ἀφανίσαντα» (μήτε with the participle governed by an infinitive); «οὐ λέγω ὡς τότε δυνάμενος» (οὐ with the participial clause in a statement) [PRIMARY examples; the rule is GENERAL].
8. **Gloss lines help only for rare words**: 2026 glossed ἄερκτος, ἐπεργάζομαι, ἀπογράφω; students still misread σηκός/μορία (the unit of the speech) because the context note explains them.
9. **Mechanical Modern Greek**: the Center for Greek Language page (translation support index) discusses "σχολική μετάφραση" as an exam skill; one article summary said many students translate mechanically into Modern Greek producing nonsense [WEAK: search summary]. Keep sentences that read as Modern Greek, but keep every ancient element (particles, tense, voice) represented.

Method tutors/ΕΕΦ imply (not a single source) [INFERENCE]: 1) find main verb(s); 2) find the introducing verb of any speech; 3) tag every participle: attributive, circumstantial (time/cause/condition/concession/purpose/manner), predicative after verbs of perception, genitive/accusative absolute; 4) conjunctions: ὅτι/ὡς/ὥστε/ἵνα/ἐπεί; 5) relative clauses; 6) μέν/δέ pairs; 7) write.

### 6.3 Taught text and parallel text (50 marks of the paper)
- The taught-text answers are **open and component-scored**. The ΕΕΦ and tutors say they presuppose **"the textbook's interpretive commentary"** (2024), but 2026's Νάτσης note that the best answers use **own words and correct terminology, not the memorized commentary**. [TUTOR]
- Β2 parallel text: required **both** texts quoted; one similarity + one difference (2025), two similarities (2024), synthesis (2026). The trap is "παράλληλη παράθεση" (writing the two texts one after the other) instead of finding **axes of convergence** (2026 Ζώτου).
- **Etymology (Β3/Β4)**: two formats: modern word → find ancient cognate in the text (2023, 2026); fill the blank with a derivative of the given ancient word (2024, 2025). ΕΕΦ 2025: the difficult ones were **οισοφάγος (< οἴσω-φαγεῖν) and φερέφωνο** (derived from φέρω with the φέρω-family). Derivation by **root, compound, prefix**, not by sound.
- **Intro questions** are literal: names, places, numbers; read the introduction in the book, not the summary.

### 6.4 Morphology traps (Γ3) [real items first, rules GENERAL]
- **Aorist β΄ imperatives**: σχές (2023), φάνηθι (2025: aorist β΄ passive imperative ends in **-ηθι**, whereas the aorist α΄ passive ends in **-θητι**: διαβλήθητι 2026). The famous irregular accents: **εἰπέ, ἐλθέ, εὑρέ, ἰδέ, λαβέ** [GENERAL].
- **Compounds of ἔχω**: προύχων → πρόσχες (2022). **Accent of compound imperatives**.
- **Attic future** of verbs in -ίζω/-άζω (ἀφανίζω → ἀφανιῶ, infinitive ἀφανιεῖν, 2026); the future of verbs like καταβαίνω → καταβήσομαι (participle καταβησόμενοι, 2023); the **middle futures** (ὄψομαι: ὀψομένη, 2025).
- **Optative of φημί**: φαίην, φαίης, **φαίη**, φαῖμεν/φαῖεν… [GENERAL; 2026 key].
- **Augment in compounds**: περιοικοῦσι → **περιῴκουν** (2026): augment after the prefix, then the contract of οἰκέω [GENERAL].
- **Perfect**: reduplication/augment (πέπεικε, ἐψηφισμένων, πεπλευκώς, κατῆχε 2024). Verbs that start with ψ, ζ, ξ, or have a prefix behave differently [GENERAL].
- **Adjectives/adverbs**: μέγας → μείζων (gen pl μειζόνων), πολύς → πλείων/πλέονα/πλεῖστος, ῥᾴδιος → ῥᾴων/ῥᾷστος, πάλαι → παλαίτερον, ἴδιος → ἰδιαίτερος (the key also lists ἰδιωτέραν: disputed).
- **Pronouns**: ὅστις → οἵτινες (not a double inflection of ὅς only), ἑαυτοῦ → ὑμᾶς αὐτάς (reflexive person changes with the pronoun, 2024), οὐδείς → οὐδεμιᾷ (feminine uses οὐδεμία, 2025), τοῦτον→τούτους, ὃς→οἵ (breathings and accents).
- **Third declension**: ἄστυ→ἄστη; γυνή→γυναικός; φύσις→φύσεως (Attic -εως); καθαρότης→καθαρότητος; πλῆθος→πλήθεσι(ν); ἐλπίς→ἐλπίδι; ἀγεννής→ἀγεννοῦς.
- **Participle pairs**: ἐπιμεληθῆναι→ἐπιμεληθέντων (masc. gen. pl. of the aorist passive participle); the "ίδια φωνή" trap for deponents.
- **Contracts** (not seen in these items but in the Gymnasium syllabus): ἀποροῦντα, γεωργῶν→γεωργούντων. Teach the contract table and the rule "the participle declines like the verb's contracted ending".

### 6.5 Syntax traps (Γ4) [real items; labels from keys, TUTOR]
- **ὡς + participle** = subjective cause (ὡς ἀφανίσαντα, causal participle), not "as".
- **οὔσης ... δημοκρατίας** = genitive absolute (temporal/causal) vs accusative absolute (ἐξόν) — textual variant issue in 2026.
- **ἀδικεῖν** = subject of the impersonal ἐξῆν (2026); **ὀνομάζειν** subject of δεῖ (2023); **ἐπικρατῆσαι** subject of εἰκός (2022): infinitives as subjects of impersonals are asked almost every year.
- **Infinitive of reference** (ἰδεῖν after εὐπρεπῆ, 2025) vs final infinitive vs infinitive as object.
- **Dative roles**: respect (πλήθει), means (μισθῷ), objective after "enemy" (τῷ Τισσαφέρνει: οἱ πολέμιοι), indirect object (μοι); **ἐμαυτῷ** (2026) as dative of interest/reference [UNSURE which label the ΚΕΕ key chose].
- **Genitives**: comparison (τῆς φύσεως after ὀρθοτέραν), objective (ἄπειρος τῶν χαλεπῶν), partitive (πάντων after superlative; ἐλαῶν/μορίων), absolute.
- **Predicate**: φίλην is the object complement «κατηγορούμενο του αντικειμένου» via ποιησάμενος; ἐπιμελητὴς via ὡς; σοφὸν (2023) via ποιήσει.
- **Predicative participle after verbs of perception** (ὁρῶ σε ἀποροῦντα) vs circumstantial: ask "can the sentence stand without it and keep its meaning?".
- **Conditionals (Γ4β 2025/2026)**: the school classification is "πραγματικό (εἰ + οριστική), προσδοκώμενο (ἐάν + υποτακτική… μέλλων), ευκταίο/δυνητικό (εἰ + ευκτική…ἄν + ευκτική), μη πραγματικό (εἰ + ιστορικός χρόνος … ἄν + ιστορικός χρόνος)". 2026's «Πῶς δ' ἄν, εἰ μὴ … ἦν, … ἐπεχείρησα» is **μη πραγματικό (past form: εἰ + imperfect … ἄν + aorist)** and the conversion to προσδοκώμενο is **ἐάν μὴ … ᾖ … + future indicative in the apodosis** [GENERAL; 2021 key shows the same pattern: «πραγματικό → προσδοκώμενο: ἐάν … συγγένηται, νομιῶ»].
- **Indirect speech (2022, 2023)**: after a historic main verb (ἔλεγεν ὅτι), the verb goes **to the optative of the same tense** (φοβεῖ → φοβοίη), 1st person pronouns shift (με → αὑτόν/ἐμέ), deictics shift (νῦν → τότε, τούτῳ…). The 2023 key printed the typo «φοβοῖ» next to «φοβοίη»; the **optative φοβοίη** is the expected form [TUTOR + GENERAL].

### 6.6 Answer format and exam-day traps
- Number every sub-answer exactly as printed (Γ3α, Γ3β…).
- In forms questions write **only the asked form** with full polytonic accent/breathing/iota subscript (unmarked accents may cost marks; Cyprus' own marking removes 0.25 per accent/breathing/subscript mistake in spelling, but this is not the Panhellenic rule) [WEAK].
- In syntax write the **governing word in Greek** (e.g. «στο ρήμα ἐπεχείρησα»).
- Write closed answers as "1: Σωστό / Λάθος" using the **full words** the paper asks (the paper says «τη λέξη Σωστό ή Λάθος»).
- Translate the **whole** marked passage (a gap in the middle of a long period is common); leave no blank line; guesses are better than blanks [INFERENCE].
- Keep the last 10 minutes to re-read the translation aloud (inner voice) for Modern Greek sense.

## 8. Learning science and motivation: only what is new for Ancient Greek

Read first: Carpe's `traps.md` §7 and `skepsis-learning-science.md` (Dunlosky, Rowland, Cepeda spacing, Pan et al. interleaving in verb morphology, Roediger & Karpicke, Jamieson, Ramirez & Beilock, Duolingo streaks, Sailer & Homner). Not repeated.

New or Greek-specific:
- **There is almost no controlled research on teaching Ancient Greek/Latin methods.** A search for experiments comparing grammar-translation with reading methods found only position papers and one correlational project (Boyd, SCS abstracts); claims about retention are argued, not measured [search summary, WEAK]. Conclusion: **do not claim the method is evidence-based beyond general retrieval/spacing/interleaving**.
- **Classics-specific classroom studies exist** (titles seen in a search, **not opened; the Cambridge pages returned 403**): *Journal of Classics Teaching* (Cambridge): "an investigation into the impact of vocabulary retrieval practice as a method of formative assessment in a Latin AS-level unseen translation context" (2024) and "The impact of using Memrise on student perceptions of learning Latin vocabulary and on long term memory of words" (2015). Consistent with retrieval-first cards; I have **no numbers**.
- **Duolingo-style design is already in the brief**; the Greek-specific risk is that students see **"55% below the base" in the news** (2026). [INFERENCE] The app's tone should frame **10/20 as the first milestone**, show a **points-by-section map** (translation 20, taught 50, grammar 10, syntax 10, comprehension 10), and make the daily goal small (5 minutes keeps the streak, as in Carpe).
- **Why the exam is stressful**: a bad day costs several 10-mark blocks; the same-day tutor commentary is mixed (Poukamisas "βατά", ΕΕΦ "demanding" for 2025). The app should avoid treating the commentary as truth; show the student that **markers accept documented alternatives**.
- **Writing practice**: because 30 marks are open interpretive answers, add a "checklist of key points" self-grading step (the student ticks the points she wrote). I found no evidence for the effect size; mark it as design intuition [INFERENCE].

## 9. What this means for the app (rules derived from the evidence)

1. **Mirror the paper (Θέμα Α1 / Β1-Β4 / Γ1-Γ4)** with the real wording ("στην ίδια φωνή", "πλήρως συντακτικά", "τεκμηριώστε με παραπομπές"), and marks per component.
