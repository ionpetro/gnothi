# Gnothi: curriculum design (Αρχαία Ελληνικά, Πανελλαδικές 31 Μαΐου 2027)

Written 2026-11-02 by the lead-designer agent from the research in this folder: `syllabus.md`, `pastpapers.md`, `texts.md` + `texts.jsonl`, `vocab.md` + `vocab.jsonl` + `vocab_names.jsonl`, `morphology.md`, `syntax.md`, `interpretation.md` + `cognates.jsonl`, `traps.md`, and the local-only `official-*.txt` files. Nothing below comes from memory: every exam fact points at one of those files, and every Greek form, label or meaning a writer puts into a unit must be checked against the school books (`source-data/` dumps of Οικονόμου's Γραμματική, Μουμτζάκης's Συντακτικό, the Εγχειρίδιο Γλωσσικής Διδασκαλίας, the Φάκελος Υλικού and Φιλοσοφικός Λόγος) before it ships. The brief named Τζάρτζανος; that is the Latin reference. For Ancient Greek the reference books listed in Υ.Α. 90176/Δ2/2026 are **Οικονόμου (grammar) and Μουμτζάκης (syntax)**.

Audience: one Greek 17-year-old in Γ΄ Λυκείου (Ο.Π. Ανθρωπιστικών Σπουδών) who follows the Φιλοσοφικός Λόγος at school, has done the Gymnasium grammar and the Α΄ Λυκείου Εγχειρίδιο, and also uses Carpe for Latin. UI language: modern Greek, the school's terminology (Οικονόμου/Μουμτζάκης/ΙΤΥΕ). Tone: warm, dry, never childish. Mascot: **Γλαύκη**, a small owl of Athena (γλαύξ) with moods happy / sad / cheer / sleepy / laurel (speed round). Brand line: «Γνῶθι σαυτόν. Γνῶθι το 20.»

Carpe is the template. Where this file says "as Carpe", the Carpe design (`../latin-panhellenic/research/CURRICULUM.md`, `app/CONTENT_SCHEMA.md`, `engine.js`) applies unchanged. This file lists only what Ancient Greek changes.

## 0. The exam in one screen (what the course optimises for)

Format fixed since 2023 (ΦΕΚ Β΄ 5136/2022; the 2023-2026 June papers follow it exactly; `syllabus.md` §4, `pastpapers.md` finding 3):

| Θέμα | Marks | What it is | Our units |
|---|---|---|---|
| Α1 | 10 | Closed comprehension on the printed reference text: Σ/Λ (15 of 16 papers), sometimes with the Greek words that prove it; «σε ποια λέξη αναφέρεται η αντωνυμία» (8 of 16); MC or matching | u2, u5, u8, u11 |
| Β1 | 10 | Interpretive question on the passage, often split 6+4 / 7+3, second part «δύο γλωσσικές επιλογές» in 5 of 16 | u2, u5, u8, u11 |
| Β2 | 10 | A modern-Greek **parallel text printed on the paper** (Orwell, Marcus Aurelius, Anonymus Iamblichi, Stathopoulos…): N ομοιότητες / 1 ομοιότητα + 1 διαφορά, quoting both texts | u2, u5, u8, u11 |
| Β3 | 10 | Etymology, 5 items × 2: find in the passage a cognate of a modern word (2023, 2026); fill gaps with cognates of a given ancient form (2024, 2025); match ancient ↔ modern (2021-22, Sept) | u1 (+ every text unit) |
| Β4 | 10 | Closed question on the book's **Εισαγωγή** (Socrates, Plato, Protagoras intro, Republic intro, Aristotle's life, EN and Politics intros): Σ/Λ, MC, matching | u13 |
| Γ1 | 20 | Translate 8-10 lines of an unseen Attic prose passage (12-20 lines printed, with a modern-Greek introduction and 2-4 glosses) | u7, u14 |
| Γ2 | 10 | Open comprehension of the unseen | u14 |
| Γ3 | 10 | Produce 6-10 forms from the unseen («το β΄ ενικό της προστακτικής αορίστου β΄ στην ίδια φωνή», «τον ίδιο τύπο στον άλλο αριθμό», degrees) | u3, u4, u6, u9 |
| Γ4 | 10 | Γ4α: label 4-7 bold words «πλήρως συντακτικά» (role + type + head word); Γ4β: one task that rotates (conditional → προσδοκώμενο 2021/2026, direct → indirect 2022/2023, participle → clause 2024, noun clause: είδος/εκφορά/ρόλος 2025) | u10, u12 |
| all | | «Κάθε απάντηση τεκμηριωμένη είναι αποδεκτή» | u14 |

Facts that drive the weighting (all from the research; counts are over the 16 papers 2021-2025 plus June 2026 unless stated):
- The **taught half is 50 marks and is a closed pool of 19 reference texts** (`texts.md` §3). The taught text is never translated or parsed in the exam (last translation: 2018). It is examined for content, pronoun referents, expressive devices, interpretation, comparison with a parallel text, and etymology of its words. So the text units drill *reading at word level* and *argument structure*, not translation.
- No June passage has repeated since 2021; 12 of the 19 have never been a June text; 6 never appeared in any paper read (Α.3, Β.7, Γ.9, Γ.10, Γ.11, Δ.15). Absence predicts nothing: **all 19 are taught with equal weight**; the app never predicts a passage.
- **Γ3 is 100 % "produce the form"** and the same ten clusters recur (`morphology.md` §11): 3rd-declension case/number change (every paper), comparison (9 of 13 recent), imperative of every tense and voice (7), future (6), aorist β΄ (6), infinitive/participle in another tense (7), optative (rare since 2021 but asked in 2026: φαίη), pronouns (every paper), perfect (3), augment after a prefix. The old 2000-2018 papers were mood-heavy (optative 12 of 19, vocative 7 of 19): still examinable.
- **Γ4α labels**: of 36 words in 2021-2026, ετερόπτωτοι (genitive/dative types) 8, objects 6, infinitives 6 (an infinitive in 6 of 6 June papers), participles 5, predicates 5, prepositional 3, subjects 3 (`syntax.md` §1.3). The marker always names the head word.
- **Γ4β rotates and never repeated in consecutive years**; not yet asked and therefore "due": active ↔ passive, clause → participle, ποιητικό αίτιο, ενδοιαστική, τελική/αποτελεσματική justification. Teach every type.
- **The unseen is never recycled** (no passage overlaps another paper by more than 5 %), but the author is predictable: 2021-2026 Lysias ×5, Xenophon ×3, Plato ×2, Thucydides ×2, Isocrates, Plutarch; June 2000-2026 Xenophon 10, Thucydides 6, Isocrates 3, Plato 3, Lysias 2. Vocabulary coverage: a frequency list of ~450 lemmas covers 78 % of the running words of the 14 past unseens, 766 lemmas 84 %; the last 13 % is a rare tail that only word-formation skills catch (`vocab.md` §1).
- **Half the candidates score under 10/20** (2026: 55.67 % of 18,977; 2025: 47.69 %; `traps.md` §3). The app treats 10/20 as a celebrated milestone and never implies that anything under 15 is failure.

Date: **Monday 31 May 2027, 08:30**, 3 hours (news republications of the ministry schedule of 11.09.2026, `syllabus.md` §3; the original PDF was not found; confirm in spring). Today is 2026-11-02: 210 days. Latin is two days later (Wed 2 June): the two apps share the week.

**Path and unlocking.** As Carpe: sequential inside a unit, but **the first lesson of every unit is open from day one**, so she can follow what her school teaches this week (the four text units u2/u5/u8/u11 follow the book's order Πρωταγόρας → Πολιτεία → Ηθικά → Πολιτικά) and still drill forms in the evening. The path screen is the **Ιερά Οδός** (the road from the Dipylon to Eleusis), 14 milestones (ἕρμαι); the roundup node of each unit is «Ανακεφαλαίωση».

**Why this order.** Etymology first because it is the lightest 10 marks and makes every later word stick (Carpe's u1 did the same). Texts alternate with grammar so neither half of the paper waits. Nouns before verbs (the Εγχειρίδιο itself starts with the 3rd declension, and nominal case/number change is in every Γ3). The unseen vocabulary (u7) sits mid-path once the declensions and principal parts exist to hang it on. Syntax Ι before ΙΙ because every Γ4β task presumes Γ4α labels. The Εισαγωγή (u13) is late only because it is pure memory and sits best in the spring; its first lesson is open from day one. Translation practice (u14) comes last on the path but its first lesson («η μέθοδος») is open from day one and the Θέμα της ημέρας pulls unseen sentences every Thursday.

## 2. Content item kinds (the schema writers fill)

All units share Carpe's file shape (`registerUnit` object with `id`, `title`, `blurb`, `exam`, `lessons`; items with `id`, `kind`, `say` ≤ 160 chars, `status`, `src`, optional `exam`, `transfer`). `status` values: `"book"` (checked in the school books: Οικονόμου, Μουμτζάκης, Εγχειρίδιο, Φάκελος, Φιλοσοφικός Λόγος), `"exam"` (a real paper or its tutor key, with the year), `"general"` (standard Attic not in the school books; constructed example), `"unsure"` (flag for the philologist). New optional field on every item: `unit_ref` (Φάκελος code `"Α.1"` … `"ΣΤ.21"`) so content can be re-weighted if the ministry changes the list.

Language rule: the fields `grc`, `forms`, `given`, `answer`, `to`, `tiles` (when Greek forms) hold **polytonic Ancient Greek exactly as the Φάκελος / Οικονόμου print it**; everything the student reads as explanation is modern Greek (monotonic). The validator distinguishes the two by the presence of breathings/polytonic accents (U+1F00-1FFF) and rejects monotonic-only text in `grc` fields longer than one word.

| kind | Fields (beyond the common ones) | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `word` | `grc` (lemma as the school grammar prints it: nouns with article `ὁ ἀνήρ`, contract verbs `ποιῶ (ποιέω)`), `forms` (dictionary string: nouns `ἀνήρ, ἀνδρός`; adjectives `ἀγαθός, -ή, -όν`; verbs the six principal parts separated by ` ; `, copy from `vocab.jsonl` `forms`), `principal_parts` (verbs: list of 6, `"—"` where none), `pos`, `gender` (`m f n m/f`), `class` (κλίση / verb class string from `vocab.jsonl`), `el` (modern-Greek meaning, senses `·`-separated, text sense first), `hook` (memory line in Greek: modern cognate, phrase, image), `etym` (list of `{el, exam: year|null}` modern cognates from `cognates.jsonl`), `trap` (the form fact that costs marks, e.g. «αόρ. β΄ ἤγαγον, προστ. ἄγαγε»), `tier` (1-3), `unit_ref` or `author` (`"Λυσίας"` …) | u2, u5, u7, u8, u11 |

| `claim` | Β1 argument trainer: `q` (the question as the committee would phrase it), `unit_ref`, `chain` (ordered tiles of the full-mark answer: `{t:"thesis"|"quote"|"gloss"|"link"|"close", el, grc?}`, 4-7 tiles), `distractors` (2-4 wrong tiles: a claim the passage does not make, a quote from another passage, an anachronism), `best` (optional: for the «ποιο επιχείρημα απαντά καλύτερα» mcq, 3 candidate one-sentence answers and `a`), `marks` (e.g. `"7+3"`), `why` | u2, u5, u8, u11 |
| `parallel` | Β2: `unit_ref`, `modern` (our own 2-4 sentence modern-Greek paraphrase of a public-domain author's view, or an original statement; never the exam's copyrighted parallel texts), `author_el` («Μάρκος Αυρήλιος, Τὰ εἰς ἑαυτόν 10.6, δική μας απόδοση»), `grc_quote` (the phrase of the reference text to pair with it), `items` (`{el, bucket}` × 3-5 statements to sort into `ομοιότητα` / `διαφορά` / `δεν προκύπτει`), `why` | u2, u5, u8, u11 |

| `trap` | `wrong`, `right`, `ask`, `why`, `cat` (`read` instruction reading · `noun` · `verb` · `syntax` · `translate` · `interpret` · `marking`) | u14, plus 1-2 per grammar unit |

`cell` keys. Nominal: `nom|gen|dat|acc|voc` + `.sg|.pl` (+ `.m|.f|.n`, + `.comp|.sup`; adverbs `adv.pos|adv.comp|adv.sup`). Verbal: `ind|subj|opt|impv|inf|ptc` + tense `pres|impf|fut|aor|aor2|perf|plup|aorp|aor2p|futp` + person/number `1sg…3pl` + voice `act|mid|pass` (e.g. `impv.aor2p.2sg`, `opt.pres.3sg.act`, `ptc.fut.nom.sg.f.mid`, `inf.fut.act`, `ind.impf.3pl.act`, `ind.perf.3sg.act`). The engine uses the prefix to tell nominal from verbal items and to build neighbour-cell distractors.

Answer normalisation (engine, `norm()`): NFD, strip every combining mark (U+0300-036F: accents, breathings, diaeresis, iota subscript U+0345), map final ς → σ, lowercase, collapse spaces, strip punctuation and movable ν in parentheses; aliases table for spelling doublets (ττ/σσ, συν-/ξυν-, εἰς/ἐς, ἀθροίζω/ἁθροίζω, γίγνομαι/γίνομαι). **Letters are never ignored.** A `form` with `strict: true` compares the full NFC string too and, on an accent-only mismatch, shows «Σωστά γράμματα, λάθος τόνος: …» and awards half credit the first time. Typed answers use a polytonic keyboard overlay (accent/breathing/subscript keys under the field) plus the «Δείξε μου πλακίδια» downgrade to `build`.

## 4. Game features (Athenian flavour, Carpe's mechanics)

- **XP = οβολοί**; 6 οβολοί show as 1 δραχμή on the profile. 10 per lesson, +5 for a perfect lesson, 3 per Στάδιο lap, 15 per mock section.
- **Daily goal** tiers: **Παῖς** (10, one lesson, ~4 min), **Ἔφηβος** (20), **Ὁπλίτης** (40). A «Ελάχιστη μέρα» of 5 minutes keeps the streak: one Θέμα της ημέρας or one review set.
- **Streak** («σερί»): weekends never break it; one free freeze («ἐκεχειρία», the Olympic truce) a week, +1 every 7 days, max 2. No hearts, no lives, no punishment.
- **Ranks and badges, from Athenian life**: Παῖς (first lesson) → Ἔφηβος (7-day streak) → Πολίτης (12 lessons) → Βουλευτής (30-day streak) → Γραμματεύς (all `form` cards of u3+u4 in box ≥ 3) → Ῥήτωρ (10 unseen passages translated) → Στρατηγός (all 19 reference texts done) → Ὀλυμπιονίκης (Στάδιο 15+) → Ἀκαδημαϊκός (u13 complete, Β4 mock 10/10) → Μάντις (10 Θέματα της ημέρας in a row) → Φιλόσοφος (a full mock at 20/20 scaled) → «Γνῶθι το 20» (all badges); Flawless → «Ἀναμάρτητος».
- **Γλαύκη moods**: happy (default), cheer (perfect / badge / streak), sad (after a miss, holding the one-breath fix on a small ostrakon), sleepy (countdown at night), laurel (Στάδιο), owl-on-coin (the profile).
- **Countdown**: header chip «210 μέρες ως τη Δευτέρα 31 Μαΐου», three phases drawn on the Ιερά Οδός: Νοέμβριος-Ιανουάριος «Θεμέλια» (learn units), Φεβρουάριος-Απρίλιος «Τείχη» (weekly mock, review), Μάιος «Παναθήναια» (review only, daily Θέμα, box-6 cards pulled forward). The date is one constant `EXAM_DATE = "2027-05-31T08:30"` flagged «επιβεβαίωσέ την τον Μάρτιο».
- **Θέμα της ημέρας** (date-seeded, 2-4 minutes, scored with the real marks of that section): Δευτέρα Γ3 (5 `form` items: one 3rd-declension, one degree, one imperative, one other-tense, one pronoun), Τρίτη Γ4 (3 `label` + 1 `clause` or `transform`), Τετάρτη Α1+Β3 (one `sentence` with Σ/Λ + `find` evidence + one anaphora tap, then 5 `hook` finds on the same passage), Πέμπτη Γ1+Γ2 (2 unseen `sentence` builds + 1 `checklist`), Παρασκευή Β4 (5 `fact`, Σ/Λ or MC as the exam), Σάββατο Β1+Β2 (1 `chain` + 1 `parallel` sort), Κυριακή mixed from the weakest boxes. Draws only from units she has started, else from the first lesson of the relevant unit.
- **Διαγώνισμα**: «Μικρό διαγώνισμα» 25 minutes = one paper scaled: Α1 (3 Σ/Λ with evidence + 1 anaphora, 10), Β1 (1 `chain`, 10), Β2 (1 `parallel`, 10), Β3 (5 `hook` finds or 1 match 5+2, 10), Β4 (5 `fact`, 10), Γ1 (3 unseen `sentence` builds from one passage, 20), Γ2 (1 `checklist`, 10), Γ3 (6 `form`, 10), Γ4 (4 `label` + 1 `transform`/`clause`, 10). Result screen: the nine bars and what to review, plus the ÷5 conversion shown as «≈ 14,2 / 20» with the ⓘ that the conversion is inferred. «Μεγάλο διαγώνισμα» = a real past paper (June 2021-2026) as a 3-hour timer with the real wording; open answers self-checked against our own `checklist`/`chain` keys cross-checked with the tutor keys in `pastpapers-raw/` (flagged as unofficial). Unlocks after the first lessons of u3, u4 and u10.
- **Τετράδιο λαθών**: every item missed twice, with its `why`; the review session takes 30 % from it.
- **Στο σχολείο είμαστε στο…**: a setting «Φάκελος: ενότητα N» (1-21) drives the Wednesday/Saturday Θέμα and the swipe set, so the app follows her class.
- **Stress design**: as Carpe (optional 2-minute «γράψε την ανησυχία» card in the last week, the «η καρδιά ετοιμάζεται» line on exam eve, no league with strangers, the opt-in «δύο αδέλφια» board shared with Carpe's). The home screen says plainly that half the candidates score under 10 and that 10 is the first win.

## 5. Spaced review and the load

Leitner boxes 1/3/7/14/30/60 days as Carpe; a miss drops to box 1; `swipe` miss one box down; from 1 May 2027 every `due` is capped at `min(due, 2027-05-24)`.

Item keys: `word` → one card per lemma (question rotates: meaning, reverse, genitive/gender, principal-parts slots, cognate pick); `form` → `(lemma, cell)`; `sentence` → id (rotates: build, anaphora tap, note mcq, find); everything else its own id.

Load estimate: ~600 unseen-core words + ~550 text words (tier 1-2 of the 19 passages) ≈ 1,150 word cards, ~800 form cards, ~200 sentences of the reference texts, ~90 unseen sentences, ~140 roles, ~110 clauses, ~90 transforms, ~185 claims, ~40 parallels, ~220 hooks, ~150 facts, ~90 traps ≈ 3,250 cards. Six exposures each to reach box 6 ≈ 19,500 graded answers over 210 days ≈ 93 a day: too many for a 15-minute day. So: (a) `swipe` carries the bulk of vocabulary (25 cards in 2 minutes, half weight), (b) text-word cards of a passage are **merged into their sentence card** after box 3 (the sentence asks a word meaning instead of a separate card), (c) tier-3 words are learnable on tap only. With that the graded load is ≈ 55 a day: a lesson gives 8-12, a review set 10, the Θέμα 5-6, a swipe set 25 (counts 12), a Στάδιο lap 8-15. A 15-20 minute day covers it; 5 minutes keeps the streak and the most urgent cards. The practice builder orders due-first, oldest-first, lowest-box-first, then Τετράδιο λαθών, then weakest boxes, and interleaves kinds (never five forms of the same lemma in a row).

Vocabulary order (`vocab.md` §7): tier-1 words of the passage she is reading (by `sources`), then that passage's tier 2, then the unseen core by `balanced_per100k` descending with Lysias weighted first (`per100k_by_author.Lysias`).

## 6. The morphology engine (`morph.js`) and accuracy rules

- Build a pure-JS generator for the regular paradigms: article; 1st/2nd/Attic 2nd/contract nouns; 3rd declension by stem class (stop, -ντ, liquid, σ-stem, vowel stems πόλις/ἄστυ/βασιλεύς) with the accent rules of Οικονόμου; adjectives of the three classes; comparison with the ο/ω rule; participles of every tense and voice; the verb system from the six principal parts (endings, augment and reduplication including after prefixes and the ει-perfects, contract vowel tables, liquid futures, Attic futures, κ-aorists and the long/short stem of -μι verbs, root aorists). Exceptions live in a hand table (the 13 irregular comparisons, the five oxytone aorist-β΄ imperatives εἰπέ ἐλθέ εὑρέ ἰδέ λαβέ plus σχές, the -ηθι/-θητι passive imperatives, compound imperative accent ἄφες/ἀπόδος/κατάθες/ἔξευρε, ζῆ/ζῆθι, γνῶθι, ἴσθι/ἴθι, δός/θές/ἕς/στῆθι).
- Regression tests: `morphology.md` §9 (the 30 verified vector groups: 2025 φανῆναι → φάνηθι, ἰδεῖν → ὀψομένη, ἄξω → ἀγάγωμεν; 2026 διαβεβλημένος → διαβλήθητι, ἀφανίζειν → ἀφανιεῖν, φησιν → φαίη, περιοικοῦσι → περιῴκουν; 2024 ἔπεισε → πέπεικε, κατῆγεν → κατῆχε; 2023 ἔχειν → σχές, ἄστυ → ἄστη, ὅστις → οἵτινες …) and the paradigms of Οικονόμου in `source-data/`.
- Hand-written `form` items always carry `answer` explicitly; `morph.js` cross-checks them in `test/` and generates neighbour-cell distractors and endless Στάδιο items. Disagreement fails the test and a human decides.
- Accent-critical pair list (`morphology.md` §10.1, traps §4): built automatically from the lemma table: any two cells of one lemma whose `norm()` collide get `strict: true` (ποιεῖ/ποίει, τιμᾷ/τίμα, λῦσαι/λύσαι, εἶπε/εἰπέ, εἶσι/εἰσί, ἴθι/ἴσθι, ᾖ/ᾗ, τίς/τις, νόμος/νομός).
- Writers never invent a form, meaning, gender, label or fact. Sources in order of authority: Οικονόμου (grammar, incl. the Κατάλογος ανωμάλων ρημάτων pp. 293-314), Μουμτζάκης (syntax labels), the Εγχειρίδιο, the Φάκελος/Φιλοσοφικός Λόγος (text, glosses, intro facts), `vocab.jsonl` / `cognates.jsonl` / `texts.jsonl` (already checked against those or against Perseus and el.wiktionary), then a real paper's tutor key (flagged: keys contain slips such as «φοβοῖ» 2023, «καταγήοχεν» 2024). Anything else is `general`; anything unconfirmed is `unsure`.
- «Seen in exam» (`exam: [year]`) only from `pastpapers.md`, `morphology.md` §1.2/§9, `syntax.md` §1.2, `interpretation.md` §4/§6.3, with the year.
- No statistics in the UI except the mark scheme and tallies phrased «στα 6 διαγωνίσματα 2021-2026 ζητήθηκε …», never a probability, never «στις Πανελλήνιες» for corpus frequencies.
- Copyright: the 19 reference texts and the unseen passages are public domain and are shipped in full (from `texts.jsonl` and Perseus). Translations, glosses, interpretive notes, parallel-text paraphrases, intro facts and model answers are **our own wording**. The Φάκελος translations/commentary, the textbook, the tutor keys and the exam's parallel texts stay in gitignored folders and are never quoted beyond a short fact.
- Display: polytonic in a serif that renders breathings and subscripts well (Gentium Plus or Noto Serif via Google Fonts, with Cardo fallback; test ᾠήθην, ᾑρημένος, ᾖ, ὦ on iOS Safari). Modern Greek UI in the sans the Carpe shell uses.
